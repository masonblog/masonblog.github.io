Law, Power & History

Reasoned Talk

Reasoned Talk shares my research into the legal histories of China and the West. Episodes follow cases, institutions, and legal ideas to ask how power is exercised, how people seek justice, and how law can hold rulers to account.

Episodes

03 Trump vs. Birthright Citizenship: Court Fight Isn’t Over

2026-10-26 · 16 min · English ·

Trump lost his birthright citizenship case at the Supreme Court, then tried again. From Wong Kim Ark to Nathan v. Trump, this episode explores who becomes an American citizen at birth and how courts can protect that right.

02 A Son’s Revenge: Filial Duty and the Law in Tang China

2026-10-25 · 14 min · English ·

Xu Yuanqing killed the official who had killed his father, then surrendered. This episode follows Chen Zi’ang and Liu Zongyuan’s debate over filial duty, punishment, and the state’s responsibility to provide justice in Tang China.

01 From China to Europe: State Power and the Rule of Law

2026-10-24 · 14 min · English ·

From Qin China to medieval Europe, this episode draws on Francis Fukuyama’s The Origins of Political Order to explore how states gain power and how law can constrain rulers.
