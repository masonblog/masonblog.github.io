Podcast 20｜United States v. Huawei, Part I: From the Meng Wanzhou Case to the RICO Conspiracy Charge

Podcast 19｜The Hugjiltu Case: Strike Hard Campaigns and the Reversal of a Wrongful Conviction

On the evening of April 9, 1996, eighteen-year-old Hugjiltu discovered a murdered woman. He asked a colleague to help report the crime, only to be identified by investigators as the killer. Just 62 days separated the crime from his execution; his acquittal came 18 years later. This episode returns to the Strike Hard era, tracing the Hugjiltu case from conviction to exoneration, examining the central principles of criminal procedure, and asking what justice delayed can leave behind.
